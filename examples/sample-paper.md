# Synthetic paper: coating durability

This is an invented demonstration document, not a published research paper.
Its measurements must not be used as scientific evidence.

## Experiment

Three coating formulations were evaluated using the same invented test protocol.
Formulation A used a binder-to-additive mass ratio of 10:1. Formulation B used
8:1. Formulation C used 6:1. Substrates were prepared identically.

## Results

In this synthetic example, formulation B retained 92% of its initial adhesion
after the stated exposure, compared with 85% for A and 78% for C. Thus B had the
highest retained adhesion among the three tested formulations in this document.
The experiment does not establish a universal optimum or performance on other
substrates.
